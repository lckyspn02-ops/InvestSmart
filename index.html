01 — The Core

INVESTING
WITHOUT THE MYTHS

01

OWN, DON'T HOPE

Investing is buying a claim on future cashflows. Stocks are ownership. Funds are baskets of ownership. The goal is not to be clever this week — it is to own productive assets long enough for compounding to do the violent, quiet work.

RISK IS NOT A VILLAIN

Volatility is the fee you pay for higher expected returns. If you cannot stomach a 30–50% drawdown, you are over-allocated to equities. Match risk to time horizon: money needed in 3 years does not belong in the S&P 500.

THE ONLY EDGE MOST PEOPLE HAVE

Time. Automatic contributions. Low fees. Broad diversification. Rebalancing once or twice a year. Everything else is entertainment dressed as strategy. Index funds exist so amateurs can stop competing with professionals.

A STARTER STACK

  • 01 Emergency fund: 3–6 months of expenses in cash or T-bills.
  • 02 Kill high-interest debt. 20% APR beats almost any market return.
  • 03 Tax-advantaged accounts first (401k match, IRA, HSA).
  • 04 Broad equity + bond mix. Increase bonds as the goal date nears.

02 — Command

FINANCIAL
MANAGEMENT

50/30/20

A blunt allocation

Needs 50%. Wants 30%. Future 20%. It is a starting grid, not scripture. If your rent is 45% of income, the grid breaks — then the job is to change the inputs, not the slogan.

NET WORTH

The only scoreboard

Assets minus liabilities. Track it monthly. Income is vanity; net worth is the plot. Include retirement accounts, taxable brokerage, home equity, and every debt. Ignore likes. Watch the line.

CASHFLOW

Pay yourself first

Automate transfers the morning payday hits. If leftover cash is what you invest, you will invest leftover effort. Treat savings like rent you owe your future self — non-negotiable, slightly annoying, always on time.

DEBT TRIAGE

Avalanche method: highest interest first — mathematically optimal. Snowball: smallest balance first — psychologically sticky. Either beats paralysis. Never refinance curiosity into a 30-year mortgage of lifestyle.

THE BORING SUPERPOWER

A written plan beats a clever opinion. Define the goal, the date, the monthly number, and the account it lives in. Review quarterly. Change the plan on purpose — not because a headline screamed.

03 — Fixed Income

BONDS ARE NOT
BORING. THEY'RE BALLAST.

A bond is a loan. You are the bank. You collect coupons. You get principal back — unless the issuer defaults. Price and yield move inversely. Duration tells you how violently price will swing when rates move.

Type

TREASURIES

Backed by the U.S. government. T-bills, notes, bonds. Benchmark for “risk-free” rate. Ideal for emergency reserves and known near-term liabilities.

Type

MUNICIPALS

Issued by states and cities. Interest is often federal-tax-free (sometimes state-tax-free). Compare tax-equivalent yield, not the headline coupon.

Type

CORPORATES

Companies borrowing. Investment-grade vs high-yield (“junk”). Extra yield is compensation for default risk. Credit spreads widen when fear arrives.

Rule

DURATION

A 7-year duration fund can lose ~7% if yields jump 1%. Long bonds amplify rate risk. Short bonds sleep better. Ladder maturities if you need cash on a schedule.

WHY BONDS STILL MATTER IN A STOCK WORLD

Dry powder When equities crash, high-quality bonds often hold or rise. That is the rebalancing gift: sell ballast, buy beaten-down ownership.

Known cash Coupons and maturities fund tuition, a house down payment, or retirement withdrawals without selling stocks in a hole.

Sleep A 100% equity portfolio is a personality test. Most people fail it at the worst possible moment. Bonds are how you stay invested.

04 — The Rails

PLATFORMS THAT
MOVE THE MONEY

Educational snapshots of widely used brokerages and fund houses. Fees, tools, and fit change. Read the latest disclosures. This is a map, not an endorsement.

VANGUARD

Funds

The index-fund cathedral. Famous for rock-bottom expense ratios and a client-owned structure. Best when you want to buy the haystack and ignore the needles.

FIDELITY

Full stack

Brokerage + retirement + zero-expense index funds + strong research. A workhorse for households that want one login for 401k rollovers, IRAs, and taxable.

SCHWAB

Scale

Huge branch network, checking-like sweep, and a deep ETF shelf. Often chosen by people who still want a human somewhere in the building.

IBKR

Pro tools

Interactive Brokers. Global markets, tight spreads, serious margin and options tooling. Powerful, dense, unforgiving to beginners. Know the interface before you size up.

ROBINHOOD

Access

Frictionless mobile trading brought millions in. Commission-free is now table stakes. The risk is behavioral: easy taps can become expensive habits. Use it as a tool, not a casino skin.

TREASURYDIRECT

Gov

The official window for I Bonds and many Treasuries. Clunky UX, pristine credit. I Bonds have purchase caps and holding rules — read them. Ideal for a slice of inflation-linked ballast.

Always verify fees, order routing, SIPC coverage, and tax forms. Platforms are pipes. Your behavior is the product.

SHARP QUESTIONS

Should I pick stocks?+

Most people should not. A total-market or S&P 500 index fund plus a bond fund beats the majority of active stock-pickers after fees. If you still want to pick, cap it at a small “play” sleeve so a bad thesis cannot wreck the plan.

Bonds when rates are high?+

Higher starting yields improve the long-run math. Short and intermediate Treasuries or a high-quality bond index can lock in income without stretching for junk credit. Match duration to when you need the cash.

How much cash is too much?+

Enough to not sell assets in a panic: typically 3–12 months of expenses depending on job stability. Beyond that, idle cash is a hidden fee called inflation. Park extras in T-bills or a Treasury money-market fund.
